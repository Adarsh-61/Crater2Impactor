Analytical Forward Cratering Mechanics

The forward model predicts the dimensions and energy of a lunar crater based on the impactor's initial conditions. The physics is governed by point-source scaling theory and dimensionless "Pi-groups", which were derived from decades of laboratory impact experiments and explosion data. The same equations are used by the inverse estimate.

By varying the parameters below, you can observe how an impactor's size, speed, and the lunar material's strength affect the final crater, and see how the process transitions from the strength-dominated regime (small craters) to the gravity-dominated regime (large craters).
