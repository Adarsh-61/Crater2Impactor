Academic Feedback and Scientific Inquiries

Crater2Impactor is an open-source planetary science research project. We welcome observations, empirical suggestions, and methodological discussions from the planetary science, impact mechanics, and simulation-based inference communities.

If you identify an anomalous posterior estimate, wish to propose benchmark lunar craters, or would like to discuss forward scaling calibrations, please submit your feedback below.
